    About Me

    I am a Visiting Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Florida A&M University (FAMU). I received my Ph.D. in Financial Mathematics from Florida State University in 2026 under the supervision of Dr. Lingjiong Zhu.

    My research interests lie at the intersection of stochastic optimization, probability theory, machine learning, and financial mathematics. My doctoral research focuses on algorithmic stability and generalization of heavy-tailed stochastic gradient algorithms, particularly stochastic gradient descent with momentum (SGDm), as well as the emergence of heavy tails in stochastic optimization algorithms.

    My broader research interests include stochastic dynamics, sampling algorithms, data science, computational finance, and financial risk management. I am also interested in developing mathematical and computational approaches to real-world problems.

    At Florida A&M University, I teach undergraduate mathematics courses and mentor undergraduate students in applied mathematics and financial mathematics research.
